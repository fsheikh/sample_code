### Poet: [Munir Niazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munir_Niazi)
### Original Title:  خوبصورت زندگی کو ہم نے کیسے گزارا
### Working Title: Beautiful Life

<div class="content-blocks">
<p>
How would today come to pass, what about tomorrow? <br>
How would we forget yesterday, a day filled with sorrow?
</p>
<p>
How many more days would we live, so much work is pending <br>
How much more misery is there to endure, before it is ending.
</p>
<p>
Lost a simple thought in some peculiar hustle <br>
Needless fretting consumed our human bustle
</p>
</div>

##### Translation: Faheem Sheikh
