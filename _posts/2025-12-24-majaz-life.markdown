### Poet: [Majaz Lakhnawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majaz)
### Original Title: بلاعنوان قطعہ
### Working Title: Life
<div class="content-blocks">
<p>
How melodious is the music of life <br>
with brightness and enlightenment rife <br>
but far away somewhere in the skies <br>
death welcomes me to end the strife
</p>
</div>


##### Translation: Faheem Sheikh
