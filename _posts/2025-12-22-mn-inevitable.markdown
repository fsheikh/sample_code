### Poet: [Munir Niazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munir_Niazi)
### Original Title: ہونی دے حیلے
### Working Title: Excuses of Inevitability
<div class="content-blocks">
<p>
Whoever was at fault, who was not <br>
Now it's pointless to talk about <br>
Gone is the time for forgiveness <br>
Inane it is in the night to cry out
</p>

<p>
What happened was inevitable <br>
And inevitability can't be stopped <br>
Once wheels are set into motion <br>
Conversations can't be dropped
</p>

<p>
Already arduous was my life track <br>
besides a collar of sorrow in my neck <br>
Already cruel were citizens of the land <br>
yet a burning desire to be a wreck
</p>
</div>


##### Translation: Faheem Sheikh
